Executive participation in M&A conference calls

Manuscript in preparation

The University of Hong Kong

Prof. Andy S. Back

About the project

This project examines target executives’ participation in M&A deal announcement calls and its relationship with investor and analyst responses. It distinguishes substantive explanations from symbolic endorsements by examining linguistic alignment, speaking roles, and Q&A participation.

My contributions

  • Structured a database of 1,800 call transcripts by speaker identity, executive role, and firm affiliation, and linked call content to transaction, stock-return, and analyst data.
  • Constructed measures of linguistic style matching, narrative concentration, and Q&A participation. Combined dictionary-based measures with prompt-based LLM text scoring to construct and validate narrative coherence and executive-participation variables.
  • Conducted empirical analyses in Python and Stata, and contributed to hypothesis development, research memos, and manuscript drafts.

Methods & tools

Python · Stata · Text analysis · Linguistic style matching · LLM-assisted coding

Related work

Back, A., An, K., & Guo, X. Visible participation, unequal voice: Target executive involvement in M&A conference calls. Manuscript in preparation.

Investor disclosure in entrepreneurial financing

Research in progress

The University of Hong Kong

Prof. Andy S. Back

About the project

This project examines how startups disclose their investor relationships in public funding announcements. The research database links PitchBook financing transactions and investor records to disclosures by startups and investment institutions.

My contributions

  • Build an original database linking financing events and investor records to public announcements, with attention to source assessment, event matching, and investor-mention coding.
  • Design and compare single-agent and multi-agent workflows for source discovery, coding, review, and independent verification.
  • Evaluate coding agreement, confidence ratings, and research time against human-coded reference labels. Refine the workflows through case-level error analysis.
  • Prototype programmatic agent coordination and workflows that combine scripted automation with AI agents for research data collection.

AI-assisted research methods

The workflow comparisons are part of this data-collection work. They examine when additional review improves coding judgments and when it perpetuates errors, using manual coding as a reference.

Methods & tools

PitchBook · Record linkage · Text coding · Python · Single-agent and multi-agent workflows

Related work

Strategic Disclosure of Investor Relationships in Entrepreneurial Financing. Database development and research in progress.

Marine-fishery carbon efficiency and spatial networks

Published in Ocean & Coastal Management

China University of Petroleum (East China)

Prof. Jiekun Song

About the project

This study examines the characteristics and evolution of the carbon emission efficiency association network of China’s marine fishery industry, including the roles of network structure, provincial attributes, and geographic proximity.

My contributions

  • Estimated marine-fishery carbon emission efficiency using a super-efficiency SBM model.
  • Constructed interprovincial spatial networks using a modified gravity model.
  • Proposed and implemented temporal exponential random graph model (TERGM) analyses in R.
  • Coauthored the resulting article in Ocean & Coastal Management.

Methods & tools

R · Super-efficiency SBM · Modified gravity model · Social network analysis · TERGM

Related work

Song, J., Guo, X., Ding, Y., & Xiao, H. (2026). Characteristics and influencing factors of the carbon emission efficiency association network of China’s marine fishery industry. Ocean & Coastal Management, 276, 108143.
